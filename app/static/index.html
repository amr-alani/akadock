    Settings

    For covalent inhibitors. Vina cannot form bonds, so the compound is docked normally and AKADock then measures how close its reactive group (acrylamide, nitrile, epoxide and so on) gets to that residue. Under about 4 Å the bond could form; much further means the pose is not compatible with covalent binding.

    Exhaustiveness 8 is the Vina default. Higher values search more thoroughly but take longer.

    Flexible side chains let those residues rotate while docking, which models induced fit. Use 3 or 4 pocket residues at most: each one makes the run slower and the scores are then only comparable inside this run.
