AKADock — terms of use

Please read this before installing.

1. WHAT AKADOCK IS

AKADock is a desktop application for molecular docking. It predicts how small
molecules might bind to a protein, using AutoDock Vina. It runs entirely on this
computer. Your structures and compounds are not uploaded anywhere.

2. WHAT IT IS NOT — PLEASE READ

Docking scores are estimates, not measurements. They are not proof that a
compound binds, and they are not proof that it is active, safe or effective.

AKADock must not be used as the basis of a medical, clinical or diagnostic
decision. It is a research tool. Results need to be confirmed by laboratory
experiments before they mean anything.

4. DATA FROM THE INTERNET

AKADock can download protein structures from the RCSB Protein Data Bank and
compound sets from ChEMBL when you ask it to. Both are public scientific
databases. This happens only when you use those features. Everything else works
without an internet connection.

This installer may also download the Microsoft WebView2 Runtime from Microsoft
if your computer does not already have it. The application window needs it.
