Methods

The protein structure (1eve.pdb) was cleaned with Gemmi (waters, alternate conformations and hetero groups other than metal ions removed) and hydrogens were added at pH 7.4 with Open Babel 3.2.1, which also wrote the receptor PDBQT. The docking box was centered on the co-crystallized ligand E20:A:2001 (center [2.022, 63.295, 67.061] Å; size [20.0, 21.3, 21.7] Å, ligand extent + 10.0 Å, minimum 20 Å). Compounds were parsed with RDKit 2026.09.1 (largest fragment kept), used as drawn (no pH adjustment), embedded in 3D with ETKDGv3 and MMFF94-minimized, then converted to PDBQT with Meeko 0.8.0. Docking used AutoDock Vina v1.2.5 with exhaustiveness 8, 9 poses and random seed 42. Protein–ligand interactions of the top-ranked pose were profiled with PLIP 3.0.1. Physicochemical properties were computed with RDKit.

Docking scores estimate binding affinity and are not proof of activity. The receptor is treated as rigid; protonation states, tautomers and ring conformations are assigned automatically and may be wrong for unusual chemistry.
